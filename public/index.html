Why judges should care

PayPal and AI, both load-bearing

PayPal isn’t a button slapped on — orders are created and captured server-side via Orders v2, recorded to a ledger, and reconcilable. AI isn’t garnish — it writes the inventory, staffs the counter, and closes the books. Remove either and the product stops working.
